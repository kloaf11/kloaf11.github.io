## About

I am Kenneth Borges, a cyber security engineer in Central Florida.

## Summary

I am a cyber security engineer with more than ten years across cybersecurity, infrastructure, databases, and data analysis. My core strength is vulnerability management carried all the way through: validating findings, planning practical fixes, coordinating remediation, and verifying closure. I bring a rare mix of hands-on Linux, Windows, identity, and database experience, and I work well with security, infrastructure, and business teams to turn findings into action. I learn unfamiliar platforms quickly, lead technical workstreams, and use Python, PowerShell, Bash, and SQL, with AI assistance that I always review, test, and validate, to automate security and infrastructure work.

## Strengths

- Vulnerability management: investigating and validating scanner findings, prioritizing by risk, and driving remediation to verified closure.
- Compliance and hardening: working with RMF, NIST 800-53, and DISA STIG requirements, including evidence collection and audit readiness, plus RHEL 9 build and hardening.
- Identity and access: Active Directory administration, privileged and service account management, and smart-card MFA and PAM on Linux.
- Database security and administration: strong SQL, PostgreSQL and SQL Server administration, access controls, backup and recovery, and disaster-recovery planning.
- Security operations support: log analysis in Splunk, technical incident-response support, evidence gathering, and root-cause troubleshooting.
- Automation: Python, PowerShell, Bash, SQL, and Git for repeatable security workflows, with independent testing of any AI-assisted code.
